# 2026-10-20: Adaptive Multidomain Lifestyle Coach (Prevention Angle)

**Opportunity**: Personalized adaptive digital coaching from Brodaty et al. 2025 Nature Medicine RCT — dynamically adjust modules/doses by adherence and progress instead of fixed schedules.

**GVU Success Criterion**: Total dose ≤ 10, ≥ 2 modules, weighted adherence ≥ 0.5, boost cognitive if progress < 0.3.

**Base**: Minimal pure-Python GVU (no external LLM). Fork-style addition of loop to a simple generator.

Smoke-test shows critique-driven dose adjustment across passes.
